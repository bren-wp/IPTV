# IPTV — Brand and marketing kit

**Product name:** IPTV  
**Positioning:** Premium cross-platform live TV discovery and playback  
**Headline:** One App. Every Screen.  
**Tagline:** Live TV, beautifully organized.

## The product in one paragraph

IPTV brings live channel discovery into a calm, premium viewing experience. Browse television channels by country or category, search instantly, mark your favorites, and open available programme guides. Watch across phones, tablets, connected TVs and Windows with familiar controls and one cohesive design.

## Key messages

**Discover more.** Browse a broad catalogue of live channel links with country, category and search tools.

**Make it yours.** Create a favorite-channel collection and choose the region you want to explore first. Basic preferences stay on your device.

**Keep watching.** Where a channel supplies multiple sources, the player can attempt recovery or another available stream following playback failure.

**Designed for every screen.** A responsive dark blue experience with touch-friendly mobile navigation, keyboard focus and TV remote navigation.

**Stay up to date.** Channel directory metadata is checked regularly with validated fallback snapshots. Live playback availability always depends on independent providers.

## Feature inventory (only implemented capabilities)

- Premium navy and electric-blue visual identity, vector TV/play logo and coherent icon family
- Search, countries, channel categories and on-demand live player
- Alternative sources and bounded playback recovery
- XMLTV schedules when available and accessible
- Favorites, recent channels and region preferences retained locally
- Periodic catalogue refresh and last-valid directory fallback
- Android, Android TV, Apple iOS Simulator, Windows, Samsung Tizen and LG webOS app targets

## Short store/listing description

Your live TV, beautifully organized. Discover channels around the world, explore categories, save favorites, and watch supported streams in one premium interface.

## Long description

Meet IPTV — a beautifully designed way to explore live television on the screens you love. Find channels by name, country or category. Build your own favorite channel collection. Discover current and upcoming programmes when guide data is available. Choose among supported stream sources, with intelligent recovery when a provider becomes unavailable.

IPTV puts discovery and viewing controls in one consistent experience for Windows, mobile and compatible TV platforms. Favorites and basic preferences are stored locally, and channel listings refresh regularly.

IPTV is a player and directory. It does not supply paid-TV credentials or host television broadcasts. Video quality, availability, geographical access, guide data and playback compatibility depend on the original providers.

## Editorial and legal guidelines

Do **not** claim that every channel works, that any content is licensed, that 4K/DRM/recording/VOD exists, or that packages are approved in app stores unless independently verified. Distinguish test/unsigned artifacts from published signed releases. Marketing never needs to name the upstream directory provider; license acknowledgements remain available in the technical docs and bundled dependency notices.
